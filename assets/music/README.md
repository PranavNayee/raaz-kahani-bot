# Background music

Drop royalty-free tracks here (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`). One is picked per story (same story = same
track), looped to length, normalised to a quiet bed and ducked under the narration.

Only use music you are allowed to use on YouTube and Instagram, for example the YouTube Audio Library
("no attribution required" tracks) or tracks with a CC0 licence. Do not use film or commercial songs.

If this folder has no audio files, the bot synthesises its own royalty-free mystery bed (minor pad, drone,
music-box plucks that lift to a warm major key after the twist).
